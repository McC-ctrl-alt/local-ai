Purpose and workforce context
Northstar Advisory Group is a fictional Canadian professional-services firm with 150 employees, Toronto headquarters, and remote staff. It
provides management, data, and finance consulting with approximately CAD 32 million annual revenue. HR records are confidential and
accessible only to authorized HR staff and managers for legitimate work purposes. This synthetic standard applies to employees; statutory
rights and the employment agreement prevail where they provide greater protection.

2. Vacation, leave and benefits
Vacation year is January 1 to December 31. Standard entitlement is 15 working days per full year, prorated in the first year; after five
completed years, 20 days. Statutory vacation pay and minimums are never reduced. Vacation is requested in the HR system at least 10
business days ahead where practicable; manager responds within 5 business days, considering coverage and fairness. Carryover is limited
to 5 days with manager approval and use by March 31; statutory entitlements follow applicable law.
 Leave / benefit
Internal process
Sick / personal
Notify manager before shift where practicable; record absence category without diagnosis. HR may request legally permitted
documentation after repeated or extended absence.
Protected/statutory
leave
Contact HR promptly; HR confirms eligibility, notice, documentation and job protection under the applicable province. Do not
require disclosure beyond what is needed to administer leave.
Accommodation
Employee or representative may request at any time. HR leads an individualized, confidential process; manager receives
functional restrictions and arrangements, not unnecessary medical details. Escalate undue-hardship questions to HR Director
and Legal.
Benefits
Eligible regular employees scheduled 30+ hours/week enroll from first of month after 30 days, subject to plan terms. Employer
pays 75% of standard health/dental premium; employee share via payroll. Life/disability coverage follows insurer terms.
Parental / family
HR provides applicable statutory and plan information; employee gives notice as required by law. Coordinate coverage and
return-to-work plan without discouraging leave.
Related controls
Employee agreement, payroll, records retention, and access removal are controlled; see Legal policy LEG-04 for contract/confidentiality rules and Tax policy
TAX-02 for payroll tax records and reimbursement documentation.
