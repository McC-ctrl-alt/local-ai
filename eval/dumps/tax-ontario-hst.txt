GST/HST transaction and return procedure
 Step
Control
1. Invoice coding
Use customer place-of-supply and service facts to determine rate; show tax registration number, taxable amount, rate and tax
separately. Taxable service supplied in Ontario is generally subject to 13% HST; confirm exceptions, zero-rating and
place-of-supply rules.
2. Expense capture
Require itemized third-party receipt, supplier, date, business purpose, tax amount and cost centre. Reconcile card feed to
approved expense report. Personal or missing-evidence items are not claimed as ITCs until resolved.
3. Monthly reconciliation
Reconcile output tax by jurisdiction, collected tax, ITCs, adjustments, credit notes, bad debts, imports and net tax
payable/refundable. Investigate differences over CAD 500 or 1% of account balance before filing.
4. Review and submit
Preparer signs checklist; independent reviewer compares return to GL and prior period; Director approves. Submit and remit
on or before confirmed due date; retain submission receipt and bank confirmation.
5. Correction
Correct errors in the next return where permitted or use prescribed adjustment process. Material errors, repeated
misclassification, or potential penalty exposure go to Director and external adviser promptly.
Reporting and remittance dates
Tax maintains CRA account details and confirms the assigned reporting period. For a monthly filer, the return and payment are generally
due one month after the reporting period. Internal target: submit by the 20th of the following month. If the 20th is not a business day, target
the prior business day. Do not assume the internal target changes statutory due dates.
3. Expense deductibility and approvals
 Expense type
Tax evidence / treatment control
Client meals and entertainment
Record attendees, client or business purpose, date and itemized receipt. Generally only 50% is deductible for income tax;
ITC recovery may also be restricted. Flag unusual events, employee-only functions and exceptions for Tax review.
Travel and mileage
Business itinerary, dates, destination, purpose, project and receipts. Commuting is personal. Mileage requires date,
origin/destination, business purpose and approved rate; Tax updates rate annually.
Home office / remote work
No employee deduction is assumed in corporate books without eligibility and substantiation. Employer-provided
reimbursement follows HR/Tax guidance and documentation rules.
Capital purchases
Items over CAD 2,500 or useful life >1 year routed to fixed assets; record in-service date, business use, class and invoice.
Tax determines capital cost allowance class/rate; do not expense solely to meet budget.
Gifts, sponsorship, dues
Identify recipient, business purpose, value and any personal benefit. Charitable donations need official receipt to support
a corporate donation claim; sponsorship advertising is separately documented.
Fines, penalties, personal costs
Code separately and flag as generally non-deductible or requiring tax adjustment. No personal expense may be
reclassified as business without evidence of business purpose.
Approval and reimbursement
Employee submits expense within 30 days. Manager approves business purpose and budget; Finance checks receipt, coding, GST/HST
and policy; items over CAD 2,500, unusual travel, related-party payments, or missing receipts go to Controller. Employees do not approve
their own expenses. See HR-03 for benefit/payroll handling and LEG-04 for vendor/client contract controls.
