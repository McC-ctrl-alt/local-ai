Filing discipline
A corporate income tax return is generally due six months after fiscal year-end. A corporate balance owing is generally due two months after year-end, with a
three-month balance-due rule potentially available to qualifying Canadian-controlled private corporations. Tax confirms Northstar’s eligibility and current statutory
dates with its adviser each year; the internal April 15 target does not extend a legal deadline.
2. GST/HST controls
Northstar files monthly GST/HST returns as an internal control based on annual taxable revenue; Tax confirms the registered reporting
period and electronic filing requirements. Accounting reconciles tax collected and input tax credits (ITCs) to the general ledger each month.
Director approves return and remittance; Treasury submits payment and saves confirmation.
