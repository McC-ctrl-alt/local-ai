3. Confidentiality and data protection
 Classify information as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Client records, employee files, credentials, security findings, and
non-public financial information are at least Confidential; sensitive personal information and authentication secrets are Restricted.
 Share only for a documented business purpose, with authorized recipients, approved storage, and least-privilege access. Use
company-managed encrypted systems; do not place Confidential or Restricted data in personal email, consumer file sharing, or unapproved
AI services.
 Before a vendor or client receives personal information, confirm contract safeguards, purpose limitation, retention/deletion, incident notice,
access controls, subprocessors, and storage/access locations with Privacy and Security.
 Report suspected loss, misdirection, unauthorized access, or disclosure immediately to Security and Privacy; preserve evidence and do
not notify an external party independently. Legal coordinates privilege, contractual notice, and regulator/client response.
